# Review S3: conductivities of bio-based insulation reported in earlier studies

Written for a course exercise. The panels, the laboratory and every value are invented.

Earlier studies of similar panels report conductivities a little apart from one another, as densities and test conditions differ. Table 1 gives the median of the values they report, dry, at mean temperatures between 10 °C and 23 °C. They are not measurements of the panels of report S1.

**Table 1. Median thermal conductivity reported in earlier studies, dry.**

| Panel | λ, W/(m·K) |
| --- | ---: |
| wood fibre | 0.039 |
| cork | 0.045 |
| hemp fibre | 0.044 |
| straw | 0.055 |
