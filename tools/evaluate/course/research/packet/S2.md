# Note S2: density and moisture of the panels tested in report S1

Written for a course exercise. The panels, the laboratory and every value are invented.

The specimens of report S1 were weighed after drying at 70 °C to constant mass and again after conditioning at 23 °C and 80 % relative humidity. Table 1 gives the oven-dry density, Table 2 the moisture content after conditioning.

**Table 1. Oven-dry density of the specimens of report S1.**

| Panel | ρ, kg/m³ |
| --- | ---: |
| wood fibre | 160 |
| cork | 110 |
| hemp fibre | 45 |
| straw | 100 |

**Table 2. Moisture content after conditioning at 23 °C and 80 % relative humidity.**

| Panel | moisture content, % of dry mass |
| --- | ---: |
| wood fibre | 9.5 |
| cork | 6.0 |
| hemp fibre | 12.0 |
| straw | 14.5 |
